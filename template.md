Here are tailored answers to those four critical questions, customized specifically for your **E-Commerce Demand Forecasting & Inventory Optimization Engine**:

---

### 1. What is the problem you are solving?

> E-commerce businesses frequently struggle with inventory imbalance. Stocking out of popular products leads to lost revenue, missed sales opportunities, and frustrated customers, while overstocking ties up valuable working capital in dead inventory. Furthermore, merchants often lack a clear, real-time connection between daily sales velocity and supplier lead times, forcing procurement teams into reactive, guessing-game purchasing decisions rather than proactive planning.

---

### 2. What tools did you use?

> * **Python:** Core programming language powering the logic, data pipelines, and application framework.
> * **SQLite & SQL:** Used to design a robust relational database schema (`Products`, `Transactions`, `Inventory`) and query structured data efficiently.
> * **Pandas & NumPy:** Essential for data wrangling, aggregating daily time-series sales, and engineering predictive features like lag variables and 7-day rolling moving averages.
> * **Plotly:** Built interactive, professional-grade visual trendlines and multi-bar comparison charts.
> * **Streamlit:** Deployed a fully interactive, web-based business intelligence dashboard complete with sidebars, metric cards, and warning alerts.
> 
> 

---

### 3. What insights did you discover / What solutions did you offer?

> * **The "Aha!" Moment:** Traditional month-end spreadsheet reviews obscure daily demand shifts. By breaking data down into daily time series and rolling windows, we uncovered how quickly high-velocity items burn through safety stock, exposing the exact gap between when a reorder is needed versus when it is typically placed.
> * **The Solution:** An integrated Inventory Intelligence Dashboard featuring an automated **Stockout Risk Radar** that instantly cross-references current stock-on-hand against supplier lead times and safety thresholds, providing early-warning alerts before shelves go empty.
> 
> 

---

### 4. How would a business benefit from your work?

> * **Money Saved:** Eliminates excess holding costs and dead stock by matching reorder quantities strictly to actual sales velocity, freeing up working capital.
> * **Revenue Protected:** Prevents costly stockouts on high-demand products, safeguarding top-line revenue and customer loyalty.
> * **Process Improved:** Replaces manual, error-prone spreadsheet tracking with automated real-time dashboards and exception alerts, enabling procurement teams to make faster, data-driven purchasing decisions.
